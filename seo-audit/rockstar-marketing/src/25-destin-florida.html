Summary. This is a local-relevance page generated by your Proximity plugin. The only hand-written text is the H1 "Destin, Florida", the sentence "All about Destin, Florida", and two links. Everything else comes from six shortcodes: [rs_weather], [rs_about], [rs_neighborhoods], [rs_location_map], [rs_thingstodo] and [rs_competitors_directions] (location_id 3). Pages like this can support local relevance for "Destin", but only if the output is unique and useful. Three risks: (1) the meta description is copied from Wikipedia ("Destin is a city located in Okaloosa County… principal city of the Crestview–Fort Walton Beach–Destin… metropolitan area"), which suggests the rs_about block may be Wikipedia text too, i.e. duplicate content; (2) rs_competitors_directions may list competing agencies and link to them; (3) the page's only ranking is #42 for "fort walton beach search engine optimization", a keyword that belongs on the FWB SEO page (FWS-09). That's probably because the competitor block names FWB SEO companies.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment, and the connector only shows the shortcodes, not their rendered output. Several items below are Verify live checks on what the plugin prints. Data comes from WordPress (connector), AIOSEO and Semrush US.
