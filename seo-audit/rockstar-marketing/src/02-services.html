URL: https://rockstar-marketing.com/services/  ·  WP ID 11534  ·  Builder: Classic editor  ·  SEO plugin: All in One SEO
Audit date: 2026-10-25  ·  Last modified in WP: 2022-09-13  ·  Page 2 of 33 in the site queue
Summary. This should be the hub that sends visitors and link equity to every service page. Right now it's a 460-word text block with zero links, no H1 or H2 in the content, no custom SEO title, and references to dead platforms and old algorithm updates (Google+ Local, Digg, StumbleUpon, "Panda and the recent Penguin updates"). Semrush shows it ranking only on page 4+ (#31 "seo destin", #51 "seo company destin fl"), so it gets no traffic. It also competes weakly with Home and /seo/ for the same SEO terms. The fix: make it the "Digital Marketing Services in Destin" hub, with one short section and a link per service, and clean up the outdated copy.

Method note: same as the Home audit. The live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector), AIOSEO and Semrush US. Verify live = check in the browser.
