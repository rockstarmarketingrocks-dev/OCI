Separate Miramar pages for each service would be thin, so the best use of this URL is one combined page: "Miramar Beach & Sandestin Digital Marketing" (social media first, plus SEO and web design). Miramar Beach is the only city on the site with no SEO or web design page. Your Destin office is only a few miles away, which also makes Miramar Beach the one city outside Destin where you can realistically appear in the map pack.

Content problems: the two paragraphs are wrapped in <span data-slate-fragment="…"> attributes containing about 3KB of base64-encoded text each (pasted from a rich-text editor). Decoded, the hidden text says "Unlike other firms in Destin", so it was copied from the Destin page. The visible copy is rambling and first-person ("I mean, maybe still seems a little crazy… we can say confidently, decisive!"), with "Miramar Beaches Best" (no apostrophe, unproven claim). The meta description is empty, the image is the shared stock photo at full 847px over http:// with alt="", and the bulleted platform list includes Reddit and Twitter. One thing is good: the only internal link goes correctly to /social-media/.
