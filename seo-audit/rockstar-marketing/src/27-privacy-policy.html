SEO Audit & Changelog: Privacy Policy

URL: https://rockstar-marketing.com/privacy-policy/  ·  WP ID 11909  ·  Builder: Classic editor  ·  SEO plugin: All in One SEO
Audit date: 2026-10-25  ·  Created / last modified: 2018-09-11  ·  Page 27 in the site queue
Summary. This page has little SEO value, but it's a blocker for the tracking plan in every previous audit. It's a generic 2018 template (about 300 words) that says "Data is never given to advertisers" and that it is "not a legally binding contract". The recommended Meta Pixel/CAPI, Google Ads conversion import, remarketing audiences, GA4 Enhanced Measurement, call tracking and Quform-to-CRM sync all share data with advertising platforms. Google Ads and Meta both require advertisers to disclose this in a privacy policy. Update this page before turning those on. It also doesn't mention cookies, forms, phone or SMS, the Google Maps and YouTube embeds, or how to request deletion, and its section labels are plain text rather than headings.

Not legal advice. The draft in §5 is a plain-language starting point that reflects the tools recommended in this audit. Have an attorney review it (especially if you text leads, serve EU/UK visitors, or process client data on clients' behalf).

Method note: data comes from WordPress (connector) and AIOSEO; the live site couldn't be reached. Which tools are actually installed must be verified live before publishing.

5. Draft Policy Text (attorney review recommended)

[Brackets] = confirm or remove depending on what's actually installed or offered.

<h1> Privacy Policy · Last updated: [Month DD, YYYY]

<h2> Who We Are
Rockstar Marketing ("we", "us") operates rockstar-marketing.com. Our office is at 385 Harbor Blvd, Suite 200, Destin, FL 32541. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your choices.

<h2> Information We Collect
<h3> Information you give us: When you fill out a form, call or email us, we collect what you provide, such as your name, email, phone number, business name, website and message.
<h3> Information collected automatically: Like most websites, we collect device and usage data (IP address, browser, pages viewed, referring site, approximate location) using cookies and similar technologies.

<h2> How We Use Information
To respond to inquiries and provide quotes and services; to operate, secure and improve the website; to measure marketing performance; and, where permitted, to show you relevant ads about our services on other sites and apps.

<h2> Cookies, Analytics & Advertising
We use Google Analytics [and Google Tag Manager] to understand how visitors use our site. [We use advertising tools from Google (Google Ads) and Meta (Facebook/Instagram), including the Meta Pixel and conversion tracking, to measure ads and show ads to people who have visited our site.] [We use call tracking to understand which marketing brings in calls.] These providers may collect information about your activity on our site and across other sites. You can manage this through your browser's cookie settings, Google Ads Settings, the Google Analytics opt-out add-on and Meta's ad preferences. [Our cookie banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies.]

<h2> Embedded Content
Some pages include embedded content such as Google Maps and YouTube videos. These services may collect data and set cookies under their own privacy policies.

<h2> How We Share Information
We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run our business (website hosting, form and email delivery, analytics, advertising and [CRM/call tracking] providers), when required by law, or to protect our rights.

<h2> How Long We Keep Information
We keep inquiry and client information for as long as needed to respond and provide services, and for up to [X] years afterward for business records. Analytics data is kept for [14] months.

<h2> Your Choices & Rights
You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you've given us by contacting us below. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. [If you text us or opt in to texts, reply STOP to opt out.]

<h2> Security
We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

<h2> Children's Privacy
This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

<h2> Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows when it last changed.

<h2> Contact Us
Questions or requests: info@rockstar-marketing.com · (850) 542-3091 · 385 Harbor Blvd, Suite 200, Destin, FL 32541 · or use our contact form.
