Summary. This page is empty. Its entire content is one link with the anchor "Locations" pointing to the home page. It has no SEO title, no description and no child pages. It was probably created as a menu placeholder. As it stands it's a thin, indexable page, and every rewrite in this audit series (Home, Services, About, Social) now links to it as "see all areas we serve". It should become the location hub: one page that links to all 11 city pages and the Destin Florida page, explains the service area, and passes authority down to the city pages that are currently losing to /web-design/ and Home for their own city keywords.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector) and AIOSEO. Verify live = check in the browser.
