SEO Audit & Changelog: Clients

URL: https://rockstar-marketing.com/clients/  ·  WP ID 4015  ·  Builder: WPBakery (full_width template)  ·  SEO plugin: All in One SEO
Audit date: 2026-10-25  ·  Last modified in WP: 2022-09-13  ·  Page 9 of 33 in the site queue
Summary. Every page we've rewritten so far links here as proof ("see our clients"). Right now the page is a 25-image gallery with zero words: no heading in the content, no client names, no results, no testimonials. The image files are named "jh", "aaaa", "sssss", "2323", "343434" and "7676", so Google can't tell what (or who) is in them. Each image also has its own indexable attachment page (e.g. /jh-3/, /clients/sssss/, /clients/attachment/7676/), which are thin pages that waste crawl budget. The fix: turn the page into a Clients & Results page (named logos, 3–5 short case studies, testimonials) and redirect attachment pages site-wide.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector) and AIOSEO. The connector doesn't expose image alt text. Given the file titles, assume alt text is empty and verify in the Media Library.
