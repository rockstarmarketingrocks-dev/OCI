Summary. This is the site's only content about its "AI Certified" positioning, which Home mentions twice. It's built as a blog post with a broken URL: /12280-2/ (WordPress used the post ID because the post was first saved without a title). The topic is commercial (a service, not an article), so it belongs as a service page. There's real national demand here: Semrush US shows "ai seo services" 4,400/mo (KD 34), "ai seo agency" 4,400 (KD 36), "ai marketing agency" 3,600 (KD 49), "ai marketing for small business" 260 (KD 27), but "destin ai" only 20.

Content: about 1,100 words that list tools rather than explain your service. It has 0 internal links, typos ("hi searched", "perspective markets", run-on fragments), outdated tool names (Wix ADI), keyword-stuffed image alts on a UUID-named file, and no meta. It also lists five certifications ("Google AI Certified, Meta AI Certified, Amazon AI Certified, IBM AI Certified, Microsoft AI Certified") without saying which certificates or showing proof. Most important, it barely mentions AI search (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity), the AI service clients now actually ask for and the one your FAQ already promotes.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector), AIOSEO and Semrush US.
