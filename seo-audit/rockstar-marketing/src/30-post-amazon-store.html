Summary. This is the most substantial post on the blog: about 1,200 words that follow Amazon's real seller journey, and it already has 4 internal links. Three things to decide or fix:
1. Business fit. Amazon isn't listed as a service anywhere on the site. If you do offer Amazon store/listing help, add it to /services/ and make this post its lead magnet. If not, keep the post as eCommerce authority content and point its CTA at Shopify/eCommerce web design and paid social.
2. Keyword. Semrush US: "amazon store optimization" = 210/mo at KD 9, an unusually easy, relevant term that matches this post exactly. The broad terms are much harder ("amazon storefront" 27,100/KD 56; "how to create an amazon storefront" 3,600/KD 53; "amazon listing optimization" 2,400/KD 40; "amazon seo" 4,400/KD 43). Target the easy one; use the others as secondary phrases.
3. Structure and accuracy. The content is crammed into paragraphs separated by <br> line breaks instead of H3s and lists; there are about 14 broken </p><p> fragments inside paragraph blocks; "Better Brand Content (BBC)" was renamed A+ Content years ago; there's a garbled line ("You can buy brands on Amazon." should be Sponsored Brands) and a typo-heading ("Look over management" should be review management); the image is the Amazon logo with no alt text; and there's no meta.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector), AIOSEO and Semrush US. Check Amazon program names in Seller Central before publishing.

5. Content Changes (ready to paste)

Keep the existing step text, restructured under the H3s in §3. New and corrected passages below.

NEW answer box after intro What is Amazon store optimization? Amazon store optimization is improving everything shoppers and Amazon's search algorithm see: your product listings (titles, bullets, images, A+ Content), your branded Amazon Store, your advertising and your reviews, so your products rank higher and convert more visitors into buyers.

Step 1 → <h3> Create Your Amazon Store Once you're enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry, you can build a free, multi-page Amazon Store (your brand's storefront on Amazon, with its own URL) using Amazon's drag-and-drop templates.

Step 2 → <h3> Descriptions & A+ Content Brand-registered sellers can replace the plain description with A+ Content (formerly "Enhanced Brand Content"): comparison charts, lifestyle images and brand story modules that typically lift conversion. Add short product videos to your listing and Store.

Step 4 → <h3> Sponsored Brands Sponsored Brands ads show your logo, a custom headline and several products (or a video) at the top of search results, and can send shoppers to your Amazon Store.

Step 4 → <h3> Sponsored Display & DSP Sponsored Display retargets shoppers who viewed your products, on and off Amazon. Amazon DSP adds programmatic display and video for larger budgets.

Step 4 → NEW <h3> Driving External Traffic Send traffic from social media ads, Instagram and your own site's landing pages to your Amazon listings. Brand Registry sellers can use Amazon Attribution to measure it, and may earn a Brand Referral Bonus.

Step 5 → <h3> Review Requests & Management Use the "Request a Review" button in Seller Central (or Amazon-compliant automation) to ask every buyer for a review. Never offer incentives for reviews; Amazon prohibits it. Respond publicly and professionally to negative feedback where you can.

NEW <h2> FAQ
Do I need Brand Registry to have an Amazon Store? Yes. Amazon Stores are available to brand owners enrolled in Brand Registry, which typically requires an active or pending trademark.
How long does Amazon listing optimization take to work? Listing changes can affect conversion within days. Organic rank usually improves over several weeks as sales velocity builds.
Should I sell on Amazon or my own website? Ideally both: Amazon for reach, your own store for margin and customer data. [We build fast Shopify and eCommerce websites.]
