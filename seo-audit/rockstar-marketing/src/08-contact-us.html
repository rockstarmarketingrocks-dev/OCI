SEO Audit & Changelog: Contact Us

URL: https://rockstar-marketing.com/contact-us/  ·  WP ID 11576  ·  Builder: WPBakery + Quform (form id 1)  ·  SEO plugin: All in One SEO
Audit date: 2026-10-25  ·  Last modified in WP: 2024-02-12  ·  Page 8 of 33 in the site queue
Summary. This is the site's conversion page. Every CTA in the previous 7 audits points here, so measurement matters more on this page than anywhere else. It's in decent shape: form, NAP, hours, email and map are all present. The problems are mainly conversion and technical. Neither phone number is click-to-call, the email address is exposed as plain text, the map iframe is fixed at 1100px wide (it overflows on mobile) and isn't lazy-loaded, there are probably two H1s, the meta description is the duplicate used site-wide, and there is no thank-you page to measure leads. There's no reassurance copy either (what happens after I submit? how fast will you reply?).

New NAP data found here: second phone (855) 545-3669 (toll-free), email info@rockstar-marketing.com, hours Mon–Fri 9am–5pm, Sat by phone, Sun closed. Use them in schema site-wide.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector) and AIOSEO. Verify live = check in the browser.

11P3Emoji bullets📍📞📩🕘 render inconsistently and screen readers announce them ("round pushpin"). Wrap them in <span aria-hidden="true"> or use icon fonts. Mark up hours as a list or table.
12P3Internal links0 links. Add a short "Not ready to talk? Explore our services" row with links to the 4 service pages and /faq/.
13P3TrustAdd a single testimonial or a Google rating snippet ("★ 4.9 from [N] Google reviews") beside the form. Use your real rating.

6. Tracking & Measurement

PriorityItemAction
P1generate_leadFire on /thank-you/ page view (GTM trigger: Page Path equals /thank-you/), with parameters form_id=1, service (from the dropdown, passed as a query string, e.g. /thank-you/?service=seo) and lead_source_page (referrer). Mark it as a Key Event.
P1phone_call_clickBoth tel: links, with parameter phone_line (main / tollfree). Mark it as a Key Event.
P1Google Ads conversionsImport generate_lead and phone_call_click from GA4 into Google Ads. Add a Google Ads call conversion for calls from ads.
P2Meta Pixel / CAPIFire Lead on /thank-you/ and Contact on tel:/mailto: clicks.
P2Form abandonmentFire form_start (Enhanced Measurement does this automatically) and compare it with generate_lead to get the form completion rate. If it's under 30%, cut fields.
P2email_click, directions_clickSecondary engagement events. Don't mark them as Key Events.
P3CRM / lead logSend Quform submissions to a sheet or CRM (Quform → webhook / Zapier) with UTM fields captured in hidden inputs, so each lead carries its source.
