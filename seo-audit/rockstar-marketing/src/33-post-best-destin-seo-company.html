Summary. This is the best-written post on the site: about 11,200 words, accurate and honest, with real Destin market insight (tourist vs. local search, seasonality, industry competition tiers, the 30A geography problem), a strong red-flag/green-flag framework and published pricing tiers ($1,000–2,000 / $2,000–4,000 / $4,000–8,000+ per month). It needs targeting and clean-up, not a rewrite:
  • Keyword overlap with Home: the title leads with "Best Destin SEO Company". Home ranks #1 for "destin seo company" (590/mo), and Semrush has no measurable volume for "best destin seo company". The guide's real search value is "how to choose an SEO company" (880/mo, KD 25), "how to choose seo company" (320, KD 32) and "questions to ask an seo company" (260, KD 14). Retitle around those and keep Destin as the angle.
  • Stuffed paragraph ×5: "Remember we serve Pensacola, Gulf Breeze, Navarre, Fort Walton Beach, Niceville, Crestview, Freeport, Destin, Miramar Beach… Florida." is pasted into the middle of unrelated sections 5 times.
  • Pasted AI chat markup: about 430 class="whitespace-normal break-words text-text-100 …" attributes (about 17KB) copied from an AI chat interface's HTML. They add weight and show where the text came from.
  • Two H1s (content + theme), 2 internal links on an 11,000-word page, no meta, Uncategorized, no images.
  • A few outdated terms: "Search Generative Experience (SGE)" → AI Overviews; "Webmaster Guidelines" → Google Search Essentials; Zomato (no longer active in the US).

Method note: the full post body (about 83,000 characters of text) was read via the WordPress connector; the live site couldn't be reached. Keyword data from Semrush US ("best destin seo company" returned no data).
