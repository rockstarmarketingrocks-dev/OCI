SEO Audit & Changelog: FAQ

URL: https://rockstar-marketing.com/faq/  ·  WP ID 12060  ·  Builder: WPBakery (single text block)  ·  SEO plugin: All in One SEO
Audit date: 2026-10-25  ·  Last modified in WP: 2025-07-30  ·  Page 11 of 33 in the site queue
Summary. This is the best content on the site: about 4,000 words and roughly 70 clear, current answers (AI search, Apple/Bing Maps, Google Business Profile, local SEO). It doesn't need a rewrite; it needs structure. Every question is a bold <span> instead of a heading, so neither Google nor AI engines can split the page into answerable chunks. There's no H1 in the content, no table of contents, no FAQPage schema, and only 4 internal links on a page that mentions every service and city. It also carries leftover ChatGPT paste attributes (data-start/data-end) and hundreds of inline color:#000000 spans. Semrush shows it ranking #25–27 for "destin search engine optimization", "santa rosa beach seo" and "seo company destin fl". That's mild cannibalization of /seo/ and Home; the fix is to point those signals at the right pages with links.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector), AIOSEO and Semrush US. Verify live = check in the browser.

4P1IntralinkingOnly 4 links (web design, SEO, social, YouTube). Add 1 contextual link per relevant answer, about 25 in total (§4). Each answer should end by pointing to the service page that sells that answer.
5P1SchemaAdd FAQPage JSON-LD (AIOSEO Pro FAQ block or a custom script). Note: since 2023 Google only shows FAQ rich results for government and health sites, but the markup still helps Google and AI engines map questions to answers. Don't add FAQ schema for the same questions on the service pages too; keep each Q&A pair marked up once.
6P2Table of contentsA 4,000-word page needs jump links. Add a TOC after the intro, linking to each category H2 (id="seo", id="ai-marketing", …). AIOSEO has a TOC block, or add anchors manually.
7P2Markup clean-upRemove ~150 <span style="color: #000000;"> wrappers (let the theme set the colour), all data-start/data-end attributes (ChatGPT paste residue), <br data-…> tags, and the empty <strong> </strong> inside the "AI Marketing" H2.
8P2"GEO SEO" terminologyIn 2025–26 "GEO" usually means Generative Engine Optimization (AI search). Your section uses it to mean geographic/local SEO, which will confuse prospects who search for GEO. Rename the section "Local SEO FAQs". Optionally add a short "What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?" Q&A to the AI section, since you offer it.
9P2Accuracy"Over 80% of local customers use Google…" has no source; cite one or soften it to "Most local customers". "Apple Maps pulls reviews from Yelp" is only part of the story: Apple Maps also has its own ratings and photos via Apple Business Connect. "WordPress powers over 40%" is fine (W3Techs ≈ 43%).
10P2Missing buyer questionsThe page has no answers on price, contracts, reporting, onboarding or results timelines for social and web. Add the 7 questions in §5.
11P2CannibalizationThe FAQ ranks #25–27 for SEO-company terms. Link the SEO answers to /seo/ ("SEO services") and Home ("Destin SEO company"), and keep commercial phrases out of the FAQ's title and H1.
12P3Accordion UXOptional: show answers in an accessible accordion (button + aria-expanded), with all answers in the HTML at load (not fetched later) so they stay indexable.
13P3Locations answerThe "What locations do you serve?" answer lists 13 towns with no links. Link the 5 city hubs and /locations/.
