SEO Audit & Changelog: About Us

URL: https://rockstar-marketing.com/about-us/  ·  WP ID 11548  ·  Builder: WPBakery  ·  SEO plugin: All in One SEO
Audit date: 2026-10-25  ·  Last modified in WP: 2023-01-04  ·  Page 7 of 33 in the site queue
Summary. An About page isn't there to rank for keywords. Its job is trust (E-E-A-T): it tells Google and prospects who is behind the business. This one names no people at all: no founder, team, history, credentials, photos or reviews. It's about 270 words of claims ("The Premier… company you will find", "99% of other firms don't work with Rockstars"). It may also have two H1s (content "About Us" + theme title), and it reuses the duplicate site-wide meta description. Its best material, the celebrity and athlete client list, is real social proof, but it's buried in a run-on sentence. The rewrite turns it into a story-led page: who we are, since 1996, the team, who we've worked with, how we work, and where we are.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector) and AIOSEO. Verify live = check in the browser. Names, bios and dates in [brackets] must come from you; I didn't invent any.

1. Priority Fix List

#PriorityAreaIssue → Fix
1P1No people (E-E-A-T)Add a founder section (name, photo, role, years of experience, short bio, LinkedIn) and a team grid. It's the single biggest trust gap on the whole site, and Google's quality guidelines look for who is responsible for a site.
2P1Duplicate H1The content has <h1><strong> About Us</strong></h1>, and the theme probably prints "About Us" as an H1 too (verify live). Keep one H1: "About Rockstar Marketing". Also remove the <strong> inside the heading and the leading space.
3P1Duplicate metaThe meta description is identical to Home, Clients and Contact. Write a unique one (§2). The SEO title is empty.
4P1IntralinkingOnly 1 link (to Home). Add links to the 4 service pages, /services/, /clients/, /locations/, /faq/ and /contact-us/ (§4).
5P2Social proofThe celebrity/athlete claims (NFL, NBA, UFC, Grammy-winning bands) are strong, but they're unnamed and unverified. Name clients where you have permission, add logos, and add 3 testimonials. If you can't name anyone, use "[N] pro athletes and touring artists" plus a testimonial.
6P2Unsupported superlatives"The Premier… company you will find", "99% of other firms". Replace with specifics: since 1996, a #1 Google ranking for "Destin SEO company", 100M+ reach, the 60M-view video.
7P2Service areaThe list mixes local towns with Mobile, Birmingham, Montgomery, New Orleans, Jacksonville and Tampa, and has no links. Replace with a linked local paragraph plus one national sentence.
8P2Copy errors"then hear the client" → "and then hear"; "Country and Rocks Bands"; "Why are we different… because" (missing question mark); "everyday" → "every day"; "Fort Walton" → "Fort Walton Beach".
9P2SchemaAdd AboutPage with mainEntity → Organization (foundingDate 1996, founder Person, address, sameAs) and Person schema for the founder.
10P3ImagesThe logo strip uses logos-1024x85.jpg (better than other pages) but has the keyword-stuffed alt "Destin SEO and Social Media Company". Cert image has alt="". Both use http://. Add team and office photos.
11P3Builder junkEmpty text block, 2 &nbsp; raw-HTML spacers. The red "WE ARE PASSIONATE…" custom-font line is fine as styling; keep it as a <p>, not a heading.
