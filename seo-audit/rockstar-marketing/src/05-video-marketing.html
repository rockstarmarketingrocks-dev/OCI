Summary. The thinnest service page so far: about 200 words, and no video on a video marketing page. Semrush: "destin video marketing" (70/mo) is won by Home at #1, with this page only at #7. There's no Destin video location page, so this page should own the local term and go after the national terms where you have real proof: "video marketing services" (2,900/mo, KD 31), "YouTube SEO services" (1,900, KD 27), "YouTube marketing services" (590, KD 23). Those are low-difficulty commercial keywords. "Destin video production" and "Destin videographer" show 0–20 searches.

Credibility problems: "YouTube's Domain Authority is 100 out of 100" (DA is a Moz metric, not a Google signal), "ranks 100 times faster" (unsourced), "rank YouTube videos in minutes" (sounds like a spam promise to anyone who knows SEO), and the 2014 stat "60 hours uploaded every minute" (now 500+).

7. Technical SEO

PriorityItemFinding / Action
P1Nested pasted markupRemove the 7 nested <div class="vc_row…"> wrappers inside the NAP text block. That's rendered builder output pasted back in, and it creates invalid, duplicate row structure.
P1VideoObject schemaFor each embed: name, description, thumbnailUrl, uploadDate, duration, embedUrl, contentUrl. Lets the page show video rich results in Google.
P2Embed performanceStandard YouTube iframes add about 500KB+ of JS each. Use a lite-youtube facade (thumbnail + click to load) or LiteSpeed's iframe lazy-load.
