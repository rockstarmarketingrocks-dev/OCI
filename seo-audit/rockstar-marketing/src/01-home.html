SEO Audit & Changelog: Home Page

URL: https://rockstar-marketing.com/  ·  WP ID 6057  ·  Builder: WPBakery (Burst theme)  ·  SEO plugin: All in One SEO
Audit date: 2026-10-25  ·  Last modified in WP: 2025-08-18  ·  Page 1 of 33 in the site queue
Summary. The home page is the site's strongest asset. Semrush (US) shows it ranking #1 for "destin seo company" (590/mo), #1 "destin seo" (210), #1 "rockstar marketing", #2 "seo company destin fl" (260), #1 "destin video marketing", #2 "destin internet marketing", #2 "destin social media company", and #7 "destin marketing agency" (110) and "destin digital marketing" (90). It wins on age and authority, not on-page quality. The page has a malformed link, empty and out-of-order headings, no H3s, outdated statistics, almost no internal links, HTTP (non-SSL) links, and generic meta tags shared with three other pages. Fix those without moving the core "Destin SEO" focus and the page should hold its #1s and push the position-7 "marketing agency / digital marketing" terms into the top 3.
Method note: The live site couldn't be reached from the audit environment, so this audit uses the page content from the WordPress database (via the site connector), AIOSEO fields, and Semrush ranking data. Items marked Verify live concern the rendered theme header/footer, scripts or speed, and need a check in the browser, PageSpeed Insights or Tag Assistant.
