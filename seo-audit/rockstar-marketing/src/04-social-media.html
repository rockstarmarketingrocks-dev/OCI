Summary. This page ranks for no social media keywords at all. Semrush only finds "santa rosa beach seo services" (#2, an SEO term) and "seo destin fl" (#85). The search intent is split: "destin social media company" (70/mo) is won by Home (#2), and the dedicated /destin-social-media-company/ page exists for local intent. Local "social media marketing Destin" searches are tiny (0–30/mo). So this page should be the general service page, targeting "social media marketing services" and "social media management services" (6,600/mo US, KD 37), while sending local intent to the city pages.

Main problems: there's no real H1, 0 links, a platform list of dead networks (Google+, Google Places, Foursquare, Digg), no TikTok, Instagram or Reels, a 29-city stuffing block, 9 empty &nbsp; paragraphs, and grammar errors in nearly every paragraph. Your best asset, reaching 100M+ people with one Facebook fan page in a month (via the large fan pages you control for cross-promotion), is buried in a run-on sentence.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector), AIOSEO and Semrush US. Verify live = check in the browser.
