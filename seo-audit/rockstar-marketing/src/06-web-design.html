Summary. This is the site's second-strongest ranking page. Semrush has it at #2–4 for nine "Destin web design" variants (e.g. "destin web design" #4 at 170/mo, "website design destin" #3, "destin website design" #2). It also shows up for Santa Rosa Beach (#9) and Fort Walton Beach (#15–17) web design terms, even though dedicated city pages exist for both. That's a sign the city pages are weak and this page is doing their job.

Decision: this page owns "Destin web design". /destin-webdesign-company/ competes for the same term and doesn't rank in the top 10. Review it in its own audit (page 14) as a candidate for a 301 redirect into /web-design/. The weak spots on this page: no real H1, 0 links, no portfolio, nested pasted markup (14 divs), a repeated city list with a typo ("Grayton Breach"), and outdated service items.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector), AIOSEO and Semrush US. Verify live = check in the browser.
