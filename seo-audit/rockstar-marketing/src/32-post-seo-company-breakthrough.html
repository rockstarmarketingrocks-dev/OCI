<h1> Do You Need an SEO Company? What They Do, What It Costs & When It's Worth It

answer box Short answer: You probably need an SEO company if customers search online for what you sell, your competitors outrank you, and you don't have the time or skills to fix it yourself. If you're brand-new with no website, or your market is tiny, start with the basics first. Here's how to decide.

<h2> What Does an SEO Company Actually Do?
<p> An SEO company improves how visible your business is in Google, Bing, Google Maps and, increasingly, AI search tools like ChatGPT, so more of the right people find you and contact you. A good one covers five areas:

<h3> Technical SEO: Fixing speed, mobile, indexing, broken links and site structure so Google can crawl and trust your site.

<h3> Keyword Research & Content: Finding the searches your customers use and creating pages that answer them.

<h3> Local SEO & Google Business Profile: Getting you into the map pack with an optimized profile, reviews and consistent listings. See our local SEO & Google Business Profile work.

<h3> Link Building: Earning mentions and links from reputable local and industry websites.

<h3> Reporting: Showing rankings, traffic, calls and leads every month, so you know what you're paying for.

<h2> 6 Signs You Need an SEO Company

  1. Competitors show up above you on Google and in the map pack.
  2. Your website gets little traffic, or traffic that never calls.
  3. You're relying entirely on referrals or paid ads.
  4. You don't know which keywords you rank for (or don't rank for).
  5. Your site is slow, outdated or hard to use on a phone.
  6. You've tried DIY SEO but don't have time to keep it up.

<h2> When You Might Not Need One (Yet)
<p> If you have no website, no Google Business Profile and no reviews, fix those fundamentals first. If you sell only to a handful of known clients, or your industry gets almost no searches, SEO may not be your best first investment. An honest agency will tell you.

<h2> Is SEO Worth It for a Small Business?
<p> For most local service businesses, yes. Unlike ads, organic rankings keep bringing in customers after the work is done, and a single extra customer per month often covers the cost. It's a medium-term investment: expect meaningful results in 4–6 months, not 4 weeks.

<h2> How Much Does an SEO Company Cost?
<p> Local SEO typically costs [$X–$Y per month] for small businesses, and more in competitive industries like legal and medical or for national campaigns. Be wary of "$99/month SEO" or anyone guaranteeing #1 rankings. [Our plans start at $X/month after a free audit.]

<h2> SEO Specialist vs. Full-Service Agency: Which Do You Need?
<p> If your site and brand are strong and you only need rankings, an SEO specialist can be enough. Most small businesses get better results from a team that can also fix the website, run social media and produce video, so traffic turns into customers. That's how we work: SEO first, with everything else in-house.

<h2> How to Choose an SEO Company (and Red Flags to Avoid)

  • Ask for real results: named clients, keywords and before/after rankings. See our client results.
  • Check their own rankings. We rank #1 on Google for "Destin SEO company".
  • Expect clear monthly reporting tied to leads, not just "impressions".
  • Red flags: guaranteed #1 rankings, secret methods, cheap bulk links, long contracts with no exit, and no access to your own accounts.

<p> Choosing locally? Read our guide on how to choose the best Destin SEO company.

<h2> What to Expect in the First 6 Months

  • Month 1: audit, strategy, technical fixes, Google Business Profile clean-up
  • Months 2–3: new and improved pages, reviews, local citations; early ranking movement
  • Months 4–6: link building and content; traffic and leads build steadily

<h2> FAQ

<h3> Can I do SEO myself? <p> Yes, the basics: a complete Google Business Profile, reviews, clear service pages. Competitive markets usually need expert help to win.

<h3> How long does SEO take to work? <p> Usually 60–90 days for early movement and 4–6 months for meaningful lead growth.

<h3> Do SEO companies guarantee rankings? <p> Reputable ones don't. Google controls rankings. Look for guaranteed transparency and effort, not guaranteed positions.

<h3> What's the difference between SEO and Google Ads? <p> Ads stop when you stop paying; SEO builds traffic that lasts. Many businesses use both. More SEO questions.
