Audit date: 2026-10-25  ·  Created / last modified: 2023-01-02  ·  Page 12 of 33 in the site queue
Summary. This page targets "Destin SEO Company" as its focus keyword, but Semrush has no rankings for this URL in the top 100. Home ranks #1 for "destin seo company" (590/mo), "destin seo" (210) and "seo destin", and #2 for "seo company destin fl" (260). Google has already chosen Home for this intent, so this page is a cannibalization risk with no upside in its current form. The copy is about 330 generic words with "Destin SEO" repeated 12 times, and its one internal link sends the anchor "Destin SEO Company" to the empty /locations/ page.

Decision: re-focus, don't compete. Turn this page into the Destin Local SEO & Google Business Profile page: map pack, GBP, reviews, citations, Apple/Bing Maps. Its job is to support Home, which owns the head term, and to fill the "GBP Optimization" backlog item from the Services audit. Six rewritten pages (Home, Services, /seo/, Clients, FAQ, Locations) already link here with the anchor "local SEO in Destin", which matches this plan. Local search volume for "local seo destin" is around 0/mo in Semrush, so expect this page to convert visitors coming from Home and the service pages, plus long-tail queries, rather than to drive large traffic of its own.
Alternative (if you don't want to maintain it): 301 → Home. It has no rankings, so a redirect loses nothing.

Method note: the live site can't be reached from the audit environment. Data comes from WordPress (connector), AIOSEO and Semrush US (no ranking data for this URL). Verify live = check in the browser.
