H1Crab Island Tours & Boat Cruises in Destin, FL

Spend the day at Crab Island, watch the sun set over Choctawhatchee Bay with dolphins swimming alongside, or catch the Destin fireworks from the water. All of it happens aboard Bikkini Bottom, a 49-passenger charter boat that leaves from 530 Harbor Blvd on the Destin Harbor. We're small, local and family-owned. Bring your cooler; we'll bring the floats, music and good vibes.

H2Destin Boat Tours Aboard Bikkini Bottom

Every trip is captained by our own crew, who know these waters well. Choose a public tour and buy tickets per person, or book the whole boat for your group.

H3Crab Island Tour (3 or 4 Hours)

Our Crab Island tours drop anchor in the middle of Destin's famous sandbar. The water is clear and waist-deep, and floating vendors, music and hundreds of boats make it one big party. Swim, float and grab food from the vendor boats while we watch the boat. Floats and toys are included. Choose a 3-hour or 4-hour trip.

See the sky turn orange over the Destin Harbor and the bay. Our Destin sunset cruise follows the routes where bottlenose dolphins feed in the evening, and most guests see them. It's a relaxed, BYOB evening that suits couples, families and anyone wrapping up a beach day.

H3How long is the Crab Island tour?

We offer 3-hour and 4-hour Crab Island tours. Both give you plenty of time to swim, float and visit the vendor boats.

H3Can I bring alcohol on the boat?

Yes. Guests 21 and over may bring their own alcohol, plus soft drinks, snacks and a small cooler. Bring water too; staying hydrated matters out there.

H3Where do I park for the boat?

Free parking is across the street at Craft Bar, 541 US-98. Don't park at the marina; vehicles there may be towed.

H3Will we see dolphins?

Wildlife sightings can't be guaranteed, but dolphins are common in Destin Harbor and most of our guests see them.

H3Can we book the whole boat?

Yes. Private charters hold up to 49 guests. Call (850) 420-2540 for group rates.
