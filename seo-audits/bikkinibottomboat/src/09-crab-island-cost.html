H2What's the Cheapest Way to Get to Crab Island?

A water shuttle is usually the lowest-cost ride, but you'll share the boat and follow its schedule. If you want a captain, floats, a restroom and time to swim without driving a boat, a public Crab Island tour is the best value. At $59.99–$74.99 for 4 hours, it's often less than a pontoon rental once you add fuel and a deposit. Book early in peak season (June–August) and pick a weekday for the best availability.
