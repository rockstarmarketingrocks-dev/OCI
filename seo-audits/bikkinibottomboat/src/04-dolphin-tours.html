H2About the Dolphins of Destin

The dolphins you'll see are Atlantic bottlenose dolphins, and many of them live in the Destin area all year. Adults grow 8–12 feet long, live 40+ years and often travel in small family groups. Researchers recognize individual dolphins by the nicks and notches on their dorsal fins. Federal law protects them, so we keep a respectful distance, never feed or chase them, and let the dolphins decide how close to come.

H2Tips for Spotting Dolphins

  • Watch for a curved dorsal fin breaking the surface, then a puff of spray as they breathe.
  • Look for diving birds; dolphins and pelicans chase the same baitfish.
  • Bring polarized sunglasses to cut glare and see beneath the surface.
  • Set your phone to burst mode; dolphins surface for only a second or two.

H2Destin Dolphin Cruise FAQs

H3Are dolphin sightings guaranteed?

No one can guarantee wildlife, but dolphins are common around Destin Harbor and most of our guests see them.

H3Can you swim with dolphins in Destin?

Not with wild dolphins. NOAA guidelines prohibit feeding, touching or swimming with wild dolphins because it changes their behavior and puts them at risk. A dolphin cruise is the best way to see them up close in their natural habitat.

H3What's the best time of day to see dolphins?

Dolphins are around all day. Morning and late-afternoon trips often have calmer water, which makes them easier to spot.

H3Is the dolphin cruise good for kids?

Yes. It's one of our most family-friendly trips: walk-on boarding, restroom onboard and lifejackets for every size. [Owner: confirm infant/toddler lifejackets.]

H3Can I bring food and drinks?

Yes. Bring snacks and drinks in a small cooler; guests 21+ may bring alcohol.
