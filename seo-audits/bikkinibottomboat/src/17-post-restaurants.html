H2Destin Restaurant FAQs

H3What food is Destin known for?

Fresh Gulf seafood, especially grouper, triggerfish, triple tail, Royal Red shrimp and oysters. Destin calls itself the "World's Luckiest Fishing Village."

H3Which Destin restaurants can you get to by boat?

Dewey Destin's Bayside on Calhoun Ave is a favorite, and several harbor restaurants have courtesy docks. [verify list]

H3Do Destin restaurants take reservations in summer?

Many harbor and fine-dining spots do and fill up fast in June–August; seafood shacks are first-come. Call ahead or use the waitlist apps.

H3Where should we eat after a Crab Island tour?

Stay on the harbor. Our dock is steps from several restaurants, so you can go straight from the boat to dinner.
