H2Parking & Getting to the Boat

H3Where is the boat located?

530 Harbor Blvd, Destin, FL 32541, on Destin Harbor. Look for the Bikkini Bottom sign and check-in booth. Map and directions.

H3Where do I park?

Free parking is across the street at Craft Bar (541 US-98). Take a left at the end of the driveway and park behind the small offices; signs will guide you. Don't park at the boat location; you may be towed. Please lock up your valuables.

H3How do I walk from parking to the boat?

Walk toward the highway, turn left to the Benning Dr. light, use the crosswalk across Highway 98 and head to the marina. Boarding is walk-on.

H3★ How early should I arrive?

Please check in [15–20] minutes before departure. Boats leave on time.

H3★ Can I get dropped off or take an Uber?

Yes. Use 530 Harbor Blvd as the drop-off address.

H2Crab Island Questions

H3★ Where is Crab Island?

Crab Island is a shallow sandbar in Choctawhatchee Bay, just north of the Marler Bridge between Destin and Okaloosa Island. What is Crab Island?

H3★ How far is Crab Island from Destin Harbor?

About a [10]-minute boat ride from our dock.

H3★ Can you get to Crab Island without a boat?

No. Crab Island is surrounded by water and can only be reached by boat: a tour, shuttle, pontoon rental or private charter. Compare your options.

H3What can I expect on a Crab Island tour?

Clear, waist-deep water, floating vendors and a party atmosphere. We anchor in the middle of the action so you can swim, float and grab food from vendor boats. See Crab Island tours.
