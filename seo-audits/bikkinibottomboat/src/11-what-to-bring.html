H2Crab Island Rules: Alcohol, Glass & Safety

Crab Island is public water managed by Okaloosa County and Florida wildlife officers, who patrol it on busy days. Plan around these basics:

  • Alcohol: adults 21+ only. Officers enforce underage drinking and boating-under-the-influence laws. (On our tours, the captain drives, so you don't have to.)
  • No glass: broken glass in shallow water is a hazard. Bring cans and plastic. [verify any current county glass ordinance]
  • Stay near your boat and away from the channel, where currents are strong.
  • Pack out your trash. We keep a trash bag onboard.
