H1Crab Island Tours in Destin, FL

Our Crab Island tours take you from Destin Harbor to Florida's most famous sandbar. You get clear, waist-deep water, floating food vendors and a party of boats at anchor. Choose a 3-hour or 4-hour trip aboard Bikkini Bottom, our 49-passenger charter boat with a restroom onboard. Floats, noodles, a floating pad and games are included. Bring your cooler and we'll handle the rest.

H2What to Expect on Our Crab Island Boat Tour

Check in at our booth at 530 Harbor Blvd and walk right onto the boat. Your captain cruises out of Destin Harbor, under the Marler Bridge and into Choctawhatchee Bay. Keep an eye out for dolphins on the way. At Crab Island we anchor in the middle of the action. Climb down the ladder into shallow, clear water to swim, float and toss a football. You can also visit the vendor boats selling food, drinks and ice cream. When it's time to go, the crew brings everyone back aboard for an easy cruise home.

Crab Island isn't really an island. It's a sandbar you can only reach by boat. Learn what Crab Island is and why it's so popular →

H2Crab Island Tour FAQs

H3Can I bring food and alcohol?

Yes. Bring your own drinks (21+ for alcohol), snacks and a small cooler. Bring plenty of water too.

H3How deep is the water at Crab Island?

Most of the sandbar is about waist-deep at low tide. Depth changes with the tide, so we provide lifejackets and floats for everyone. Is Crab Island safe for kids? →

H3Is the tour wheelchair accessible?

Boarding at the marina is a level walk-on. At Crab Island, though, getting in and out of the water means using a ladder, so we can't consider the excursion handicap accessible.
