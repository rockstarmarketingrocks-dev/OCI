H2Destin Sunset Cruise: What to Expect

Check in at 530 Harbor Blvd about 15 minutes early and walk right onto the boat. Your captain heads out through Destin Harbor, past the docks and boardwalk, toward the East Pass and the bay. Our captains know where dolphin pods feed in the evening and slow down when they see them. Most of our guests see dolphins. The sun sets over the water on the way back, so have your camera ready; our crew is happy to take group photos. The trip is relaxed and BYOB, and there's a restroom onboard. It's great for couples, families with kids, and groups of up to 49 guests.

H2Dolphins in Destin Harbor

Destin's waters are home to Atlantic bottlenose dolphins all year. They often feed near the harbor mouth and the East Pass in the evening. We can't guarantee wildlife, but our captains have a good track record of finding them. By law we watch from a respectful distance and never feed or chase dolphins. Want more dolphin time in daylight? Try our daytime dolphin tour in Destin.

H2Destin Fireworks Cruise
