## Fastest alt-text fix
Rank Math > General Settings > Images: turn on "Add missing ALT attributes" and set the format to `%title% %count(alt)%`. Every image with an empty alt then gets the page title (which now starts with Crab Island) plus a number. That covers Rank Math's "focus keyword in image alt" check.
