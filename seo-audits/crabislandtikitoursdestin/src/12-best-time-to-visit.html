5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

H2: Best Time for Clear Water (new)

Mornings are usually clearer, because fewer boats and swimmers have stirred up the sand. But no single hour guarantees that famous emerald water. Tide, wind and recent weather all change visibility from day to day. An incoming tide that pushes Gulf water through the East Pass often helps, and a strong south wind or recent rain can cloud things up. See high tide vs low tide at Crab Island for more.

Confirm the incoming-tide point with your captain before publishing. It is commonly said locally, but this audit could not verify it.
