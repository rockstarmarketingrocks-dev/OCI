Crab Island Tiki Tours: Site-Wide SEO Summary

Site: crabislandtikitoursdestin.com  |  Audit dates: October 28 to 29, 2026  |  27 queue items: 25 changelogs, 2 posts covered by merges

This summary pulls together the issues that repeat across pages. Fixing these once clears a large share of every page changelog. Page-level detail is in files 01 to 27.

1. Decisions only the owner can make

#QuestionWhy it matters / where it appears
1What are the real prices?Private charter "from" $800, $900 or $600; sunset $400 or $600; public $30, $50, $59.99 or $60. At least 8 pages disagree (Home, Charter, Sunset, Bachelorette $900/$1,800, Hub, Cost, FAQ, guides).
2Does the base price cover 8 guests with a per-guest add-on, and how much is it?Stated on Hub, Cost, Pontoon and FAQ pages; contradicted by "$900 split 18 ways is $50".
3Which trip lengths exist?Pages list 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 hours in different combinations. The "2 Hours" icons on cards may be real.
4Which phone number and email are correct?Contact page shows 850-613-1966 but dials 850-543-6378; shows a Gmail address but opens a Yahoo address.
5What's onboard?Floats, inflatable mat, one or several paddle boards, water toys: pages disagree. "Captain and crew" vs "captain".
6Cancellation, weather and late-arrival policy wordingFour versions on the FAQ page alone, more on Bachelorette, Best Time and How to Get. Draft merged text is in the FAQ changelog.
7Who writes the guides (author byline)?Captain Bill, Daniel or Spencer.
8Public tour page: comparison page or noindex?Avoids competing with bikkinibottomboat.com.

3. Technical setup

  • Rank Math Local SEO: LocalBusiness with name, 530 Harbor Blvd Suite #13, phone, geo, hours, sameAs, matching Google Business Profile.
  • Product/TouristTrip schema with Offers on the Charter and Sunset pages only.
  • FAQPage schema via Rank Math FAQ blocks on the guides and FAQ page.
  • Article/BlogPosting with a named author and dateModified on guides and posts.
  • Breadcrumbs once /charters/ is live.
  • Check for a second H1 from the theme on posts.

4. Tracking setup

  • FareHarbor GA4 integration (both shortnames) and cross-domain measurement with fareharbor.com; mark purchase as a key event.
  • Contact Form 7 wpcf7mailsent to GA4 generate_lead key event.
  • Click events: book_now_click (with FareHarbor item ID and button location), phone_click, email_click, directions_click, outbound_click.
  • GA4 content groups: product, guide, blog, company.
  • UTM-tagged links in Google Business Profile and the FareHarbor confirmation email.

5. Suggested order of work

  1. Owner decisions in section 1 (prices, phone, email, policy).
  2. Contact page links, broken URLs and redirects, slugs.
  3. FareHarbor GA4 and cross-domain tracking.
  4. Synced blocks and price/policy updates across all pages.
  5. Home, Charter, Sunset and Bachelorette rewrites (the pages that sell).
  6. Guide and post fixes, schema, images.
