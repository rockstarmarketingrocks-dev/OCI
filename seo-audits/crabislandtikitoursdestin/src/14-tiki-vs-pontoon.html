2. Critical fixes: the cost argument

Both a pontoon rental and a private charter are priced per boat. Splitting either one across more people lowers the per-person cost by the same proportion. Group size alone never flips which is cheaper. What flips it is capacity: once a group outgrows one pontoon (10 to 14 people, per this page), it needs two rentals, two drivers and two fuel bills, while one tiki boat still fits 18.

5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

Quick answer (revised)

A pontoon rental costs less and puts you at the wheel: typically $300 to $500 for a half day or $500 to $800 for a full day in Destin, plus fuel. Anyone driving who was born on or after January 1, 1988 needs a Florida boater safety card. A captained tiki boat costs more and removes the driving, docking, anchoring and fuel. It also holds more people. Our 32-foot tiki boat seats 18, while most rental pontoons hold 10 to 14. For small groups the pontoon is usually cheaper. Once your group would need two pontoons, one tiki boat often costs about the same.

Revised FAQ: Is a tiki boat worth it compared to a pontoon rental?

It costs more for the same group, so it depends on what you value. The extra buys a licensed captain, fuel, a bathroom, full shade, room for 18 and a day where nobody in your group has to drive. For four adults with an experienced boater, a pontoon is the better deal. For a big group, a celebration, or a trip with kids and grandparents, most people find the charter worth it.

New FAQ

Can I rent a pontoon in Destin without a boating license? Florida doesn't issue a "boating license," but anyone born on or after January 1, 1988 needs a boater safety education card to operate a boat with 10 horsepower or more. Rental companies will ask for it. On a captained charter, no card is needed. FWC boating education.
