5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

Facts below come from this page and the Sunset and Charter pages. Items in italics need the owner's confirmation.

H1 and intro

H1: Crab Island Bachelorette Party in Destin, FL

Get the whole bride tribe on one private tiki boat and spend the day at Crab Island. Bring your own drinks, play your own playlist on our Bluetooth speaker, and float in the shallow water while a licensed captain handles the boat. There's room for up to 18 guests and a bathroom onboard, so nobody has to leave the party.

H2: Why Bachelorette Groups Book Our Tiki Boat

  • Private boat: it's only your group, no strangers.
  • BYOB-friendly: bring your favorite drinks. We supply a cooler with ice and water.
  • Bathroom onboard: a big deal on a 4 to 8 hour trip.
  • Room for 18: one boat instead of splitting the group across two pontoon rentals.
  • No driving: your captain anchors, watches the weather and gets everyone back to the harbor.

Split across 18 guests, a 4-hour charter at $900 works out to $50 per person. (Update if the price changes.)

H2: Bachelorette Charter Packages

H3: 4-Hour Crab Island Bachelorette Charter (from $900)
The most popular choice. Plenty of time to swim, float, take photos and visit the floating vendors.

H3: 8-Hour Full-Day Bachelorette Charter (from $1,800)
Make the boat your home base for the day: Crab Island, a cruise through Destin Harbor and stops along the shoreline.

H3: Add a Sunset Cruise
Planning a night out too? Book our private sunset cruise for golden-hour photos before dinner, or choose the 6-hour Crab Island and Sunset Charter for both in one trip.

H2: What's Included

  • Licensed captain (and crew, if a crew member is actually onboard)
  • Private tiki boat with seating for up to 18
  • Bathroom onboard
  • Bluetooth speaker
  • Water toys and floats
  • Cooler with ice and water

H2: How a Bachelorette Day at Crab Island Works

H3: Meet at Destin Harbor
Bring your coolers and bags to the dock. Your captain gives a quick safety briefing and you're off. Crab Island is about half a mile away.

H3: Party at the Sandbar
The captain anchors in shallow water, usually 2 to 4 feet deep. Hop in, pull out the floats and start the playlist.

H3: Head Back (or Catch the Sunset)
Cruise back to the harbor at the end of your charter, or book the sunset combo to end the day with photos on the water.

H2: Bachelorette Planning Tips

H3: When to Book
Weekend dates in spring and summer go first. Reserve as soon as you know your date, then share the confirmation with the group. Read the best time to go to Crab Island for crowd and tide timing.

H3: What to Bring
Drinks in cans or plastic, snacks, sunscreen, water shoes, towels and cash for the floating vendors. Full list: what to bring to Crab Island.

H3: Photos and Themes
Matching swimsuits and sashes photograph well against the green water. (Add the decoration policy: are banners, balloons or confetti allowed? Most boats ban confetti.)

H2: Bachelorette Party FAQs (Rank Math FAQ block)

Can we bring alcohol? Yes, the boat is BYOB-friendly. No glass. (Add age rule if any.)

How many people fit? Up to 18 guests.

Can we decorate the boat? (Owner to answer.)

Is there a bathroom? Yes, onboard.

What is your cancellation policy? Full refund or credit with 7 days' notice. If we cancel for weather or other unforeseen reasons, you get a full refund or credit. No-shows are charged the full price. Call us to cancel.

What if the bride is a non-swimmer? Most of Crab Island is 2 to 4 feet deep. See how deep the water is at Crab Island.

H2: Book Your Crab Island Bachelorette Party

Pick your date and charter length in the calendar. Questions about your group? Contact us or call (phone). Replace "Dates book fast, especially weekend." with this block; the current sentence is missing an "s".
