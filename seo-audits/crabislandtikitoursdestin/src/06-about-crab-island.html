H1 and author line

H1: Crab Island, Destin: A Local Captain's Guide

By Captain Bill, Crab Island Tiki Tours. Updated (month, year). We run charters to the sandbar from Destin Harbor every week of the season. The depth, timing and cost figures below come from those trips.

Link "Captain Bill" to the Captain Bill page (renamed from /elementor-863/). Confirm he is the right author before publishing.

H2: Crab Island at a Glance (replaces Key Takeaways list)

QuestionAnswer
What it isA shallow, submerged sandbar, not a dry island
LocationNorth of the Marler Bridge, Choctawhatchee Bay, Destin, FL (about 30.394 N, 86.512 W)
Distance from Destin HarborAbout half a mile, 5 to 10 minutes by boat
Water depthRoughly 2 to 4 feet over most of the sandbar at normal tide
AccessBoat only (no road, bridge or walkway)
Cost to visitFree. You pay only for the boat.
VendorsFloating food and drink vendors in summer
Best quiet timeWeekday mornings in May, early June and September

A table like this is easy for Google's featured snippets and AI answer engines to quote. Keep the Quick answer paragraph above it, minus the price.

H2: High Tide vs Low Tide at Crab Island (new)

Tide changes how deep the sandbar runs and how strong the current feels. At low tide the sandbar is shallower and easier for kids. At high tide the water is deeper. (Add specifics from post 924 so the two pages agree.) Your captain checks the tide chart and picks an anchoring spot to match. Read our full high tide vs low tide guide.

H2: Is Crab Island Good for Families? (new)

Yes, with a plan. Shallow water, life jackets in child sizes and a bathroom onboard make a private charter one of the easier ways to bring kids. Watch for the deeper channel edges and keep little ones in life jackets. Read everything parents should know before visiting.

New FAQs (add to the existing five)

Can you bring alcohol to Crab Island? Guests 21 and older can bring alcohol on our charters with valid ID. No glass on the boat.

What happens if the weather turns bad? If we cancel for weather, you get a full refund or credit. You can also cancel with 7 days' notice for a full refund or credit. No-shows are charged in full.

H2: Beyond Crab Island: More to Do in Destin (new, short)

Staying a few days? Pair your sandbar day with an evening private sunset cruise, or see our list of things to do in Destin besides Crab Island. Visiting in the shoulder season? Here's why fall is a great time to visit Crab Island.
