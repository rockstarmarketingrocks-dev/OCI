Your captain makes the call based on conditions on the water. Hurricane season runs June 1 through November 30. See our FAQs for the full policy, and read about visiting Crab Island in the fall.

Wording fixes

  • Six em dashes in the resource list (for example after "National Hurricane Center"). Swap for colons.
  • "Boats and equipment can be repaired or replaced, but people cannot." Strong line; keep.

Longer term

Turn the general prep advice and resource list into an evergreen page, "Hurricane Season and Your Crab Island Trip", and update it each year. Keep this post as the record for Isaias and link it to the evergreen page. Future storm posts can then be short updates that point to the evergreen guide.
