5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

H2: Crab Island Packing Checklist (new)

Screenshot this or download the printable checklist (PDF).

  • Swimsuit (wear it), cover-up or T-shirt, dry change of clothes
  • Reef-safe sunscreen SPF 30+, face stick, hat, polarized sunglasses
  • Towel for each person
  • Water shoes or strap sandals
  • Dry bag: phone, wallet, keys, medications
  • Waterproof phone pouch and portable charger
  • Drinks in cans or plastic (no glass), water, snacks or lunch
  • Cash in small bills for floating vendors
  • Floats or toys (remove if provided onboard)
  • Decorations for celebrations (no confetti or glitter)
  • ID for anyone drinking alcohol (21+)

H2: What to Wear (new)

Wear your swimsuit to the dock, since there isn't much room to change on board. Bring a light cover-up or T-shirt for the ride back and for any restaurant stop on longer trips. A long-sleeve swim shirt protects better than sunscreen for kids and anyone in the water for hours. Pack a dry change of clothes in a dry bag if you have plans after the trip.

H3: Alcohol and the No-Glass Rule (revised)

Guests 21 and older can bring alcohol with valid ID. Use cans, plastic bottles or pouches. Glass isn't allowed on our boat, and most Destin operators ban it too, because broken glass in shallow water where people walk barefoot is a real hazard.

H2: Packing for Kids (new)

Bring water shoes, a rash guard or long-sleeve swim shirt, extra sunscreen, snacks they'll actually eat and a small float or snorkel mask. We carry child-size life jackets, but bring your own if your child is used to it. Most of the sandbar is 2 to 4 feet deep. Read how deep the water is at Crab Island and is Crab Island family friendly? before you go.
