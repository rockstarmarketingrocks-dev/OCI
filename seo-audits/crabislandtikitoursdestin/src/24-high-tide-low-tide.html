5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

Quick answer (new, under H1)

Short answer: The incoming tide, in the hours leading up to high tide, usually brings the clearest water to Crab Island because clean Gulf water flows in through the East Pass. On a falling tide, darker bay water moves through and the sandbar gets shallower. Wind, rain and weather matter too, so no tide guarantees perfect water.

H2: High Tide vs Low Tide at a Glance (new)

New FAQs

How many high tides does Destin get a day? (Check the NOAA chart for the Destin station. Much of the northern Gulf coast often has one high and one low tide a day rather than two, which makes timing easier. Confirm before publishing.)

Can I ask the captain to time our trip around the tide? Yes. Mention it when you book, and we'll suggest a start time that fits the tide if the schedule allows.

Does the tide affect how deep the water is? Yes, the sandbar is shallower at low tide and deeper at high tide. See how deep the water is at Crab Island.
