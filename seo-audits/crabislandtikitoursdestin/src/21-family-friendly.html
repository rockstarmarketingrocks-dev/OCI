5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

H2: Is Crab Island Family Friendly? The Short Answer

Yes, if you plan for it. Most of the Crab Island sandbar is 2 to 4 feet deep with a soft sand bottom, so kids can stand, splash and float while adults stay close. The things to watch are boat traffic, the deeper channel edges and the sun. A morning weekday trip on a boat with shade and a bathroom makes it much easier.

H3: Boat Traffic and Channel Edges (replaces the "not an actual island" point)

Crab Island isn't a beach. It's a sandbar in open water with boats coming and going all day. The edges drop off quickly into the channel, where the current is stronger. Keep young children within arm's reach, keep them away from moving boats, and stay on the shallow side. Read more about how deep the water is at Crab Island.

H2: What Makes a Private Boat Easier With Kids (new)

  • A real bathroom onboard. Most rental pontoons don't have one, which ends a lot of family trips early.
  • Full shade under the tiki roof for naps, snacks and sunburn breaks.
  • Child-size life jackets carried on every trip.
  • A captain who anchors in shallow water away from the channel, so parents can watch the kids instead of the boat.
  • Your schedule. If the kids are done after three hours, head back.

See our private Crab Island charters, or compare a tiki boat vs a pontoon rental.

H2: Crab Island With Kids: FAQs (new)

Is there a minimum age? (Owner to answer, including infants.)

Do you have life jackets for kids? Yes, in child sizes. Bring your own if your child is used to a specific one.

Is there a bathroom on the boat? Yes, a full bathroom onboard.

Can grandparents come too? Yes. There's shaded seating for everyone, and the water is shallow enough to wade in.
