5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

H3: 1. Shuttle or Public Tour Seats (revised)

Shuttle and public tour boats sell individual seats on a set schedule, usually 3 to 4 hours at the sandbar. Expect about $50 to $75 per adult. A captain and crew stay aboard, larger boats have restrooms, and most let you bring a cooler. Our sister company, Bikkini Bottom, runs a 4-hour public Crab Island tour. See public Crab Island tours.

It's the best fit for couples, solo travelers and small groups who want the lowest cost per person. You share the boat, though, and you leave when the schedule says.

H3: 2. Pontoon Rental (revised sentence)

Florida requires anyone born on or after January 1, 1988 to carry a boater safety education ID card to operate a boat with 10 horsepower or more (FWC boating education). Many rental companies offer a temporary certificate test on site. See tiki boat vs pontoon rental for a full comparison.

Confirm the temporary certificate detail with FWC before publishing. It's a common rental practice in Florida, but this audit could not check the current rules.

New FAQ

Is there a ferry to Crab Island? No public ferry runs to Crab Island. Shuttle and public tour boats fill that role, selling individual seats from Destin Harbor. See public Crab Island tours.

Revised FAQ answer: Do I need a boating license?

Only if you drive the boat yourself. Florida requires a boater safety education ID card for anyone born on or after January 1, 1988 who operates a vessel with 10 horsepower or more. On a captained charter, the USCG-licensed captain runs the boat, so nobody in your group needs one.

H2: When to Book (replaces "Planning Ahead Pays Off")

Peak season runs Memorial Day through Labor Day, and weekends sell out first. Book a private charter 3 to 7 days ahead, earlier for holiday weekends. Same-day openings happen, so call 850-613-1966 for last-minute dates. (Insert the same cancellation policy block used on the Bachelorette and FAQ pages.) For quieter days, see the best time to visit Crab Island.
