H2: The Easiest Way to Spend a Day at Crab Island

Crab Island is a shallow sandbar just north of the Marler Bridge, about half a mile from Destin Harbor, and the only way to get there is by boat. Our private tiki boat takes the work out of it. A USCG-licensed captain handles the driving, anchoring and the trip back, so your group can swim, float and relax in 2 to 4 feet of clear water.

The boat is yours for the whole trip. There's room for up to 18 guests, shade under the tiki roof, and a bathroom onboard, which matters a lot after a few hours on the water. Choose a 2-hour visit or stay up to 8 hours and add a cruise along the harbor.

Keep the existing six-item checklist under this paragraph (captain, 18 seats, bathroom, family friendly, celebrations, private tour).

9. Tracking

Site Kit by Google is installed. Booking happens on FareHarbor, so without extra setup GA4 records the Book Now click as an exit and never sees the sale.

#SetupSteps
1Confirm Site Kit connectionsSite Kit > Settings: GA4 property and Search Console both connected; GA4 tag placed by Site Kit only once (no second hard-coded gtag or GTM copy).
2FareHarbor GA4 integrationIn the FareHarbor dashboard, add the same GA4 Measurement ID under its Google Analytics integration so bookings send a purchase event with value. Do this for both shortnames (crabislandguideservice and, if you want credit for referrals, bikkinibottomboat).
3Cross-domain measurementGA4 Admin > Data streams > Configure tag settings > Configure your domains: add crabislandtikitoursdestin.com and fareharbor.com so sessions do not split at checkout.
4Key eventsMark purchase, generate_lead (Contact Form 7 submit) and phone_click as key events in GA4.
5Click eventsUse Google Tag Manager (or Site Kit's event settings) to fire book_now_click with parameters tour_name, fareharbor_item (643424, 712850, 744100, 746740, 753976, 615407) and button_location (hero, tour_card, final_cta). Fire phone_click on tel: links from WP Call Button, and outbound_click for Bikkini Bottom links.
6Ads pixels (if running ads)Add Google Ads conversion and Meta Pixel IDs inside FareHarbor's integrations so purchases are attributed. Import the GA4 purchase key event into Google Ads.
7Search Console linkLink Search Console to GA4 to see home page queries next to bookings.
