5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

Intro (under H1, above Quick Info)

See Destin from the water as the day winds down. This private 1.5-hour cruise on our tiki boat takes your group through Destin Harbor and into Choctawhatchee Bay at golden hour, with time to look for dolphins and watch the sun set over the Emerald Coast.

The boat is reserved for your group alone, up to 18 guests. Your USCG-licensed captain drives, and you bring the drinks and snacks. We supply a cooler with ice, a Bluetooth speaker and a bathroom onboard.

H2: Your Evening on the Water (replaces "The Perfect Ending to Your Day!")

H3: Destin Harbor
Board at Destin Harbor and cruise past the docks and waterfront on your way out.

H3: Choctawhatchee Bay and Dolphin Spotting
Dolphins are often seen around the harbor and the bay. Sightings are never guaranteed, but your captain keeps watch and will slow down when they appear.

H3: Sunset Over the Emerald Coast
Time your cruise with the sunset and watch the sky change color from the water. It's a good time for group photos, so bring a phone with a full battery.

Replace the current paragraph ("Embark on a serene... enchanting... magical hues"). It uses vague travel-brochure words and an em dash, and it says little a visitor can act on.

H2: What's Included on the Sunset Cruise

  • USCG-licensed captain
  • Private tiki boat for up to 18 guests
  • Bathroom onboard
  • Bluetooth speaker
  • Cooler with ice (BYOB-friendly, no glass)

H2: What Time Does the Sunset Cruise Leave? (new)

Departure times change through the year to match sunset. In summer, cruises leave in the early evening. In fall and winter, they leave earlier. Pick a date in the calendar above to see that day's start time. (Add a short table of typical start times by month once confirmed.)

"What time is the Destin sunset cruise" is a common search. Answering it on the page helps both Google and AI answers.

H2: Who Books a Private Sunset Cruise (new)

Couples celebrating an anniversary or proposal, families who want an easy evening outing with kids, friend groups starting a night out, and bachelorette parties looking for sunset photos. For a full day at the sandbar first, see our private Crab Island charters.

H2: Destin Sunset Cruise FAQs (keep the 5 existing, add 3)

Is the sunset cruise private? Yes. Keep current answer.

How long is it? Keep current answer (1.5 hours).

How many people can come? Keep current answer (up to 18).

Can we bring food and drinks? Keep current answer, add "no glass".

Will we see dolphins? Keep current answer.

Where do we meet? (New. Dock name and address at Destin Harbor, parking notes.)

What happens if it rains or storms? (New. One-line weather policy and link to the FAQs page.)

Can we combine Crab Island and the sunset? Yes. The 6-hour Crab Island and Sunset Charter includes an afternoon at the sandbar and a sunset cruise back. See charter lengths.

H2: Want More Time on the Water? (replaces the 13-word H2)

Put the 6-hour Crab Island and Sunset Charter first in the cross-sell row. It's the closest upgrade for someone already looking at sunset trips.
