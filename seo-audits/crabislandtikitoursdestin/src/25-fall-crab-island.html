5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

Quick answer (new, under H1)

Short answer: September and October are two of the best months for Crab Island. The water is still warm, crowds drop sharply after Labor Day, especially on weekdays, and the summer heat eases. The tradeoff is that it's still hurricane season, so keep an eye on the forecast.

H2: What About November? (new)

By November the water is usually too cool for a long day of swimming, but sunny afternoons on the boat are still pleasant. A private sunset cruise or a shorter harbor trip is a good fit. For other ideas, see things to do in Destin besides Crab Island.
