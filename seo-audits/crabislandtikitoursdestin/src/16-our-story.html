Our tiki boat is 32 feet long and seats up to 18 guests under a full tiki roof. It has cushioned seating, an open layout and one of the roomiest bathrooms you'll find on a boat this size. Every trip includes a USCG-licensed captain, life jackets in adult and child sizes, a Bluetooth speaker and a cooler with ice and water. (Add engine, year built or other specs if you want to.) See our private Crab Island charters and sunset cruise.

H2: What Guests Say (new)

Add the same Trustindex Google reviews widget used on the Home page, or 3 short quotes with first names and dates. Reviews next to the founders' story are strong trust signals.

Wording fixes

  • "The difference now? They get to share that experience with thousands of visitors every year." Fine if accurate. If you have a real number ("more than 2,000 guests in 2026"), use it.
  • The copy has 5 em dashes. That's a style choice, but if you're trying to avoid an "AI-written" feel, swap them for commas or periods.
  • The HTML has data-start/data-end attributes like the FAQ page. Paste as plain text when editing.
