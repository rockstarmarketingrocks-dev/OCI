2. Strategy decision first

bikkinibottomboat.com already targets "Crab Island tours", "Crab Island tickets" and "Crab Island shuttle". If this page chases the same terms, the two sister sites compete for the same results. In my opinion the better play is to make this page a comparison and referral page: "Public tour or private charter? Here's how to choose," with the public option clearly sent to Bikkini Bottom. That gives it its own search angle ("crab island public vs private", "cheap way to get to crab island") and helps both businesses.

Alternative: if the owner wants Bikkini Bottom to own every public-tour search, set this page to noindex and keep it only as a landing spot for the Home button. Pick one before rewriting.

H1: Public Crab Island Tours in Destin, FL

Don't need a whole boat? Our sister company, Bikkini Bottom Boat Tours, sells individual seats on Crab Island, sunset dolphin and fireworks trips from Destin Harbor. It's an easy, lower-cost option for couples, solo travelers and small groups. Bikkini Bottom is a separate company and handles its own bookings, so questions about public tours go to them at (850) 420-2540.

H2: Public Tour or Private Charter: Which Fits Your Group?

PricePer seat, from $59.99 for Crab IslandPer boat, from $800 for 4 hours
