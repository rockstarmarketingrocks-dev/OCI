5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

H2: Captain Bill at a Glance (new)

  • On the water in Destin since 1975
  • U.S. Coast Guard 100 Ton Master's license since 1984
  • Emerald Coast resident since 1968 (Fort Walton Beach, then Destin from 1976)
  • 39+ years with the U.S. Department of the Air Force, now retired
  • B.S. in Applied Mathematics, M.S. in Computer Science

H2: From Deckhand to Master Captain

Bill started as a deckhand in Destin in 1975 and earned his U.S. Coast Guard 100 Ton Master's license in 1984. He has worked as a deckhand and captain on these waters for more than 50 years, through decades of change at the harbor and the sandbar.

H2: A Career of Service (revised)

In 1986 Bill began a career with the U.S. Department of Defense, Department of the Air Force, that lasted more than 39 years. He kept captaining the whole time, on weekends, holidays and vacation days. Today he's retired from the Air Force and spends that time on the water instead.

Replaces "Although he dedicated his professional career to serving our country, the water was never far away," which reads as a contradiction. Confirm the retirement wording.

H2: Why Guests Ride With Captain Bill (new)

Bill knows how the tide moves across Crab Island, where the sandbar drops off, and where dolphins tend to show up in the pass. Ask him about how Destin has changed since the 1970s. (Add a short quote from Bill, or a guest review that mentions him by name.)

Wording fixes

  • "an unmatched understanding of its waterways" to "a deep understanding of its waterways". "Unmatched" can't be backed up.
  • Two em dashes in the copy. Swap for periods if you're aiming for a plainer style.
