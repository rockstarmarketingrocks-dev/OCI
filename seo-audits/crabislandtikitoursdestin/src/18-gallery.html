2. Critical fixes

#IssueFix
1P1 Photos may be invisible to GoogleCheck the live page source for <img tags inside the gallery. If there are none, switch from the Elementor (Pro) Gallery widget to the "Basic Gallery" widget or individual Image widgets, which output real <img> tags with alt text.
2P1 File names shown to visitorsThe lightbox title comes from each image's Media Library title. Rename the titles (and ideally the files) to short descriptions, e.g. "Bachelorette group at Crab Island". Fill in alt text and captions at the same time.
3P1 No text or linksAdd a short intro, section headings, captions and a booking button (section 5).
4P2 Meta description too longShorten (section 3).

5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

Intro under H1

These are real photos from our private tiki boat trips out of Destin Harbor: families floating at Crab Island, bachelorette groups, birthdays and evening sunset cruises. Every trip has room for up to 18 guests, a USCG-licensed captain and a bathroom onboard. See our private charters.

Section intros (one line each, with a link)

Our Tiki Boat: Our 32-foot tiki boat has shaded seating for 18 and a full bathroom. Read our story.

Days at Crab Island: The shallow, clear sandbar just north of the Marler Bridge. What is Crab Island?

Bachelorette Parties and Birthdays: Bring your playlist and your crew. Bachelorette charters.

Families on the Water: Most of the sandbar is 2 to 4 feet deep. Is Crab Island family friendly?

Sunset Cruises: Golden hour on the Emerald Coast. Private sunset cruise.

Share Your Photos: Tag us on Instagram or Facebook (add handles) and we may feature your trip here. Ask permission before posting identifiable guests.

Caption examples

  • "Bachelorette group floating at Crab Island, July 2026"
  • "Tiki boat anchored at the Crab Island sandbar"
  • "Sunset over Destin Harbor from the tiki boat"

Write captions only for what each photo actually shows. Dates and group types help both visitors and image search.
