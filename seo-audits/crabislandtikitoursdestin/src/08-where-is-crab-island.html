Crab Island is the shallow, light-green area just north of the Marler Bridge. Our boat leaves from 530 Harbor Blvd Suite #13 on Destin Harbor, about half a mile away. Get directions to the dock.

H2: Getting to Destin (merged)

H3: Driving In on Highway 98 and I-10
Highway 98 runs along the Emerald Coast through Destin. From the north, take I-10 and head south through Crestview, Niceville or Freeport. From Miramar Beach and 30A, Destin is 20 to 40 minutes west on 98. From Fort Walton Beach and Okaloosa Island, it's 20 to 30 minutes east across the Brooks Bridge. Panama City Beach is about an hour each way, so most visitors from there book a 6 or 8 hour charter. Traffic on 98 through Destin backs up between noon and 6 p.m. in summer, so leave extra time.

H3: Flying In: VPS, ECP and PNS
The closest airport is Destin-Fort Walton Beach (VPS). Northwest Florida Beaches International (ECP) is about an hour east, and Pensacola International (PNS) about an hour and a half west. Private aircraft use Destin Executive Airport (DTS).

New FAQ

Is there a Crab Island address? No. The sandbar has no street address because it sits in the water. Use the coordinates 30.394, -86.512 for marine navigation, or 530 Harbor Blvd Suite #13, Destin, FL to reach our dock.

Wording fixes

  • "Dolphins work that pass constantly" to "Dolphins are often seen in the pass."
  • "in a harbor full of similarly named operators" is a strong, specific tip. Keep it, and make sure the Google Business Profile name and address exactly match this page.
