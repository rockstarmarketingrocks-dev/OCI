5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

Intro under H1

Quick answers about booking, prices, the boat and Crab Island itself. Jump to: Booking · Cancellations · Prices · The Boat · Trip Options · What to Bring · Safety · Celebrations · Crab Island. Can't find it? Call 850-613-1966.

H2: Cancellations, Rescheduling and Weather (single policy block)

Our policy: Cancel or reschedule at least 7 days before your trip for a full refund or credit. If we cancel for weather or another reason, you get a full refund or credit, or we'll reschedule. Cancellations inside 7 days and no-shows are charged in full. Late arrivals may get a shorter trip, since the next group's time can't move. To cancel or reschedule, call 850-613-1966.

Do tours run in light rain? Usually yes. Summer showers often pass quickly. Lightning, strong wind and small craft advisories cancel trips, and the captain makes the call based on conditions.

Confirm the wording with the owner. It merges what the FAQ, Bachelorette and guide pages currently say. Once approved, save it as a synced pattern.

8. Technical

#ItemAction
1P1 FAQPage schemaUse one FAQ block per section, or a single block, so FAQPage JSON-LD is output. Google limits FAQ rich results to authoritative government and health sites, so don't expect stars in search. The structured Q&A still helps AI answer engines and Google understand the page.
2P2 Jump linksGive each H2 an ID (booking, cancellations, prices, boat, trips, bring, safety, celebrations, crab-island) for the jump links.
3P2 Leftover attributesThe HTML is full of data-start/data-end attributes, which usually come from text copied out of an AI chat tool. They are harmless for SEO. Paste as plain text when rebuilding.
4P3 URL/crab-island-tiki-tours-faqs/ is fine. Keep.
