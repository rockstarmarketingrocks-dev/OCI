5. Content rewrites (paste-ready)

Facts below come from the site: tiki boat, up to 18 guests, USCG-licensed captain, bathroom onboard, Destin Harbor departure, card lengths and prices as currently listed. Confirm prices in FareHarbor before publishing.

Intro (under H1)

Spend the day at Crab Island on a tiki boat that's reserved for your group alone. Your USCG-licensed captain drives, anchors and handles the trip back from the sandbar, so everyone else can swim, float, play music and relax in the clear, shallow water.

The boat seats up to 18 guests, has shade under the tiki roof and a bathroom onboard. Choose 4, 5, 6 or 8 hours, all leaving from Destin Harbor. It works for family vacations, birthdays, reunions and bachelorette parties.

H3: 4-Hour Private Crab Island Charter
A half day at Crab Island with time to swim, float and visit the vendors before heading back to the harbor. The most popular choice for families and first-time visitors.

H3: 5-Hour Private Crab Island Charter
One more hour on the sandbar. Good for groups who want a slower pace, a long lunch on the water or more time to explore.

H3: 6-Hour Crab Island and Sunset Charter
Spend the afternoon at Crab Island, then cruise the Emerald Coast and look for dolphins as the sun goes down. Two trips in one booking.

H3: 8-Hour Full-Day Crab Island Charter
A full day with your own captain. Visit Crab Island, cruise Destin Harbor and stop along the shoreline. Built for big groups and celebrations.

H2: Check Dates and Book Your Charter

Pick a date in the calendar to see open start times for each charter length. Your booking reserves the whole boat for your group. Your confirmation email includes the meeting spot at Destin Harbor and what to bring.

H3: Boarding at Destin Harbor
Meet your captain at the dock, load coolers and bags, and get a quick safety briefing. (Add check-in time, for example "Please arrive 15 minutes early," once confirmed.)

H3: Anchoring at the Sandbar
Crab Island is about half a mile from the harbor, just north of the Marler Bridge. Your captain anchors in shallow water, usually 2 to 4 feet deep depending on the tide, and your group is free to swim, float and visit the floating food and drink vendors.

H3: Cruising Back (or Staying for Sunset)
On longer charters, your captain can cruise the harbor and shoreline before returning. On the 6-hour trip, you'll head out for sunset and dolphin watching on the way in.

H2: What's Included With Every Private Charter

  • USCG-licensed captain for the full trip
  • Private use of the tiki boat for up to 18 guests
  • Onboard bathroom
  • Shaded seating under the tiki roof
  • Bluetooth music (confirm; the intro mentions music)

H2: What to Bring on Your Charter

Sunscreen, water shoes, towels, snacks, drinks in cans or plastic (no glass) and cash for the floating vendors. See the full Crab Island packing list.
